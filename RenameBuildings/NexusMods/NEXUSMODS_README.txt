[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
Rename Buildings lets you rename most player-owned buildings in Kenshi.

It was primarily created for renaming workstations so they are easier to identify at a glance. For example, you can rename one Cooking Stove to "Cook Dustwich" and another to "Cook Food Cube". Most player-owned building-type objects without doors can also be renamed.
Selecting a supported building displays a small draggable Rename button. Clicking it opens a compact rename window where you can enter a custom name. Renamed buildings keep their names permanently, including after saving and loading.

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Rename most player-owned buildings and workstations.[/*]
[*]Permanent names that persist across saves.[/*]
[*]Draggable Rename button.[/*]
[*]Button only appears for supported player-owned buildings.[/*]
[*]Toggle the rename window by clicking the Rename button again.[/*]
[*]Ownership is verified before renaming.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]RE_Kenshi (latest version)[/*]
[*]Kenshi 1.0.65+[/*]
[/list]
[heading]Limitations[/heading]
Buildings identified as doors (such as wall gates and building shells) cannot currently be renamed.

[heading]Troubleshooting[/heading]
[list]
[*]Make sure RE_Kenshi is installed and enabled.[/*]
[*]If the Rename button does not appear, verify the building is player-owned and is not identified as a door.[/*]
[*]Blank or whitespace-only names are rejected.[/*]
[*]If the new name is not immediately visible, select another character or building to refresh the displayed name.[/*]
[/list]
